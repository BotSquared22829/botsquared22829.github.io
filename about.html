We’re BotSquared.

We’re FTC Team 22829 from Killarney Secondary. We started as a group of rookies with a shared curiosity for robotics and a lot to learn. Together, we took our first ideas from sketches to a robot we could call our own.

Along the way, we grew as much as our robot did. Every build, test, and problem gave us a chance to learn something new and get better at working together. Bit by bit, a team of beginners became a strong team that could take on challenges together.

We still bring that same curiosity to everything we make. We share ideas, help each other improve, and keep finding reasons to try again. We’re proud of how far we’ve come, and excited for what we’ll build next.
