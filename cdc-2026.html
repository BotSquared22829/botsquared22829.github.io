What changed in 2026

Our 2025 Fire Truck rolled into our first competition on omni wheels, a white claw perched on its tall metal arm. That robot took second place. For 2026, we switched to four rubber Rhino wheels and tucked a 3D-printed fan housing between the front pair. The claw changed too. We drew two hooked fingers in CAD, gave each its own servo, and built a tool to tune how far each finger opens and closes. Our driver squeezes one trigger to open both fingers and releases it to grip. The same gamepad moves the arm and reverses the fan.
