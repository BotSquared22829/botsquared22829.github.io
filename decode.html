DeCode

FTC 2025

Our first FTC competition.

DeCode marked our first time competing in FIRST Tech Challenge. It was our introduction to bringing robot design, building, and programming together for an FTC competition, and to taking on the challenge as a team. This was the beginning of our FTC journey.

Introduction to CAD.

DeCode was the first time we used CAD to design our robot’s ramp and intake system. It introduced us to planning these mechanisms digitally, thinking about how their parts would fit together and how the ramp and intake would work as one system.

The season begins.

DECODE began with the game reveal on September 6, 2025, introducing the challenge for the season ahead.

Our first version.

V1 was our first version of DeCode, ahead of the first qualifier. More build details to come.

First quals.

We competed at the L.A. Matheson Qualifier in Surrey on December 6, 2025, earning the Reach Award.

The next version.

After our first qualifier came V2, the next version of DeCode. More build details to come.

Second quals.

At the Port Moody Qualifier on January 31, 2026, we earned another Reach Award and finished as part of the finalist alliance.

On to provincials.

We competed at the British Columbia Championship in Surrey on February 21–22, 2026, bringing DeCode to the provincial stage.
